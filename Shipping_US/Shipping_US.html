Strategic Takeaways

  1. Invest in Technology in the South/Central regions — Technology is 40% of West sales but only 30% in the South. Targeted expansion could unlock $80-120K in additional annual revenue.
  2. Launch a Corporate loyalty programme — Corporate customers generate the highest per-customer value ($2,917) but number only 236 accounts. Retention and upsell could yield 10-15% value growth.
  3. Optimise inventory allocation by region — Align stock with regional demand: Technology in West, Office Supplies in South/Central, Furniture in East. Reduces carrying costs and improves turnover.
  4. Revitalise the Furniture category — At 32.6% cumulative growth (vs 50%+ for other categories), Furniture needs product range refresh, pricing review, or bundle promotions.
  5. Improve Central region delivery — Slowest delivery (4.06 days) may impact repeat business. Investigate warehouse or carrier options.
