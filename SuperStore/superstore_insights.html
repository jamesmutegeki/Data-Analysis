Data Quality & Anomalies Report

Fixed $50,000 Sales Adjustments

11 records (0.11%) across 4 unique orders contain artificially capped sales values of exactly $50,000. These span all three product categories: Furniture (5), Office Supplies (5), and Technology (1). Geographically concentrated in the West (7) and South (4) regions. Affected sub-categories include Tables (2 instances), Bookcases, Chairs, Labels, Storage, Furnishings, Art, Phones, Binders, and Appliances.

These records inflate total revenue by $550,000 (from $2,285,729 to $2,835,729), but contribute only $120.26 to profit, indicating they are likely data entry caps or system overrides rather than genuine transactions.

Extreme Single-Order Losses

1 catastrophic loss event was identified: Order CA-2020-108196 in the Machines sub-category (East region) posted a -$6,599.98 loss at a 70% discount.

Beyond this extreme outlier, 12 sub-categories operate at a net loss. The worst offenders are Binders (-$38,510), Tables (-$32,412), and Machines (-$30,119). These categories collectively drain $186,199 from the bottom line, representing 65% of total gross losses.

Margin Leakage Deep Dive: The Discount-Profit Death Spiral

The Critical Threshold: 21%+ Discount

Orders discounted at 21% or more generate negative average profit. The inflection point is stark: below 20% discount, average profit per order is +$24.74; at 21-30%, it flips to -$45.68. At 41-50% discount, each order loses an average of -$298.70.

Highest-Risk Categories

Binders carry the highest average discount at 37.2% and are the #1 loss leader (-$38,510). Machines average 30.6% discount (-$30,119). Tables at 26.1% (-$32,412). These three categories are the primary margin bleed points and should be the first targets for pricing reform.

The Revenue Illusion

High-discount orders (30%+) generate -$97.18 avg profit per transaction, while low-discount orders (under 10%) earn +$66.90. The company is effectively paying customers to take inventory in loss categories. Total net profit of $286,397 represents only a 10.1% margin on gross revenue, well below the industry benchmark of 15-20% for retail.

Actionable Executive Recommendations

1

PRICING: Enforce Discount Guardrails

  • Implement a hard 20% discount ceiling on Binders, Tables, and Machines, the three categories where discounts consistently produce net losses.
  • Require manager approval for any discount above 15% on Furniture items.
  • Expected impact: Eliminating discounts above 20% on loss categories could recover $60,000-$80,000 annually in margin.
2

SUPPLY CHAIN: Optimize Regional Fulfillment

  • Standard Class shipping averages 5.0 days across all regions with minimal variance. Investigate whether this meets SLA commitments.
  • Same Day shipping shows near-zero delay (0.0-0.1 days), confirming reliability. Expand Same Day availability in the Central region where it currently has the lowest adoption.
  • Negotiate carrier contracts for the Central region, which shows the longest Standard Class delays (4.99 days).
3

INVENTORY: Rationalize Loss-Making SKUs

  • Conduct a full SKU-level profitability audit on Tables and Bookcases (Furniture). These sub-categories lose money even at moderate discount levels, suggesting cost-of-goods issues.
  • Consider discontinuing or repricing the bottom 5 loss-making sub-categories: Binders, Tables, Machines, Bookcases, and Chairs (combined loss: -$123,000).
  • Reallocate shelf space and marketing budget toward Copiers (+$55,618), Phones (+$44,516), and Accessories (+$41,937), which have the strongest margins.
4

CUSTOMER TIERING: Segment-Based Incentive Programs

  • Consumer segment drives 58.4% of revenue ($1,652,792) and 46.8% of profit. Develop a loyalty program to increase repeat purchase frequency and reduce discount dependency.
  • Corporate segment contributes 26.6% of revenue but 32.1% of profit, indicating higher-margin transactions. Expand B2B sales outreach and volume pricing tiers.
  • Home Office is the smallest segment (15.1% revenue) but maintains healthy margins. Target with premium product bundles to increase average order value.
